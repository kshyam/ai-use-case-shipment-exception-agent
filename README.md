## Overview

**Shipment Exception Agent** is a reference AI workflow for Transportation & Logistics organizations. It addresses a practical customer service challenge:

> Teams investigate delays, damage and missing shipments across carriers, warehouses and route data.

The workflow is triggered when **Shipment exception is detected**. It coordinates Shipment, carrier and resolution agents and keeps people responsible for decisions that require judgment, exception handling, or a sensitive business action.

## Why This AI Workflow Matters

Organizations often have the data and systems needed to solve this problem, but the information is spread across teams and operational tools. An AI agent workflow can help bring the right context together, explain the next step, and carry out approved actions without hiding the decision trail.

This is an architecture pattern, not a promise of automatic results. Production implementation depends on data quality, system access, workflow evaluation, security controls, and a clear human review design.

## How It Works

1. **Investigate shipment status**
2. **Combine carrier and warehouse data**
3. **Analyze the route**
4. **Recommend resolution**
5. **Communicate with the customer and open a claim if needed**

## AI Agents Involved

Shipment, carrier and resolution agents.

## Human Oversight and Security Controls

**Human checkpoint:** Approve compensation, claims and exception handling.

Recommended controls for this AI agent use case:

- Shipment permissions
- Compensation limits
- Customer communication review
- Claims audit

These controls define what the AI system can see, recommend, or do, and when a person must review the outcome. High-impact actions should fail closed when identity, policy, or approval information is missing.
